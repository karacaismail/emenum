# Add Getting Started Tutorial for New Users

## Overview

README.md covers installation and environment setup but lacks a step-by-step tutorial guiding a new restaurant owner through their first complete workflow: registration, creating organization, adding products with prices, generating QR codes, and publishing their menu.

## Rationale

The target audience includes restaurant owners who may not be technical. A guided tutorial reduces onboarding friction and ensures users understand the platform's unique features like the immutable price ledger and table-based QR codes. The QA acceptance criteria in spec.md states 'Register-to-QR-download flow completes in under 10 minutes' but there's no guide showing this flow.
